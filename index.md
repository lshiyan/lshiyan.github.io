Master's student in Computer Science at [McGill](https://www.mcgill.ca/) and [MILA](https://mila.quebec/en), supervised by Prof. [Reihaneh Rabbany](https://mila.quebec/en/directory/reihaneh-rabbany).

I'm broadly interested in agents, specifically multi-agent systems. The use of multiple agents necessitates study on how interactions between agents can shape their capabilities. Recent years have shown just how powerful these multi-agent systems can be, (looking at the Huggingface Incident as an example), but it is still an open question as to how to effectively train these systems, especially with mechanisms such as credit assignment and communication being both paramount to the function of these systems, as well as being entirely non-obvious in execution. 

I'm using this site as a stream-of-consciousness to write about cool things that interest me, ranging from academic topics (mostly AI and STEM related things), to current events and anything else I have in mind. I find that forcing my thoughts on paper, (or in this case Markdown), helps me to both consolidate my understanding of certain topics, as well as seek out more knowledge about them. I hope you find my rambling interesting.
